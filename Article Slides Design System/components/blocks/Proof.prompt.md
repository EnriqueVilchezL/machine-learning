Numbered theorem/definition/proof environment from MyST `{prf:*}` directives.

```jsx
<Proof kind="theorem" number={1} title="Convergence">
  <p>Every bounded monotone sequence converges.</p>
</Proof>
<Proof kind="definition" number="2.1">…</Proof>
```

No icon; the label is the capitalized kind plus enumerator, title in parentheses.
