MyST admonition (`{note}`, `{tip}`, `{warning}`…); use for one callout per slide, never as decoration.

```jsx
<Admonition kind="tip" title="Take-home">Pin your environment.</Admonition>
<Admonition kind="warning" simple>Preliminary data.</Admonition>
```

Colors: note/important/seealso → blue (info); tip/hint → green; attention/warning/caution → amber; danger/error → red. Icons are Heroicons exactly as MyST maps them.
