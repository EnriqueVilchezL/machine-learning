The running header line at the top of a slide, taken from the MyST frontmatter header (subject | venue … badges).

```jsx
<SlideHeader subject="Results" venue="Conference 2026" number={7} total={14} />
```

- Light weight (300), 24px, small-caps subject separated from venue by a 1px rule.
- `right` accepts badges such as `<Icon name="science/open-access" />`.
